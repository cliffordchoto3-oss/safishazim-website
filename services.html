Beyond the catalogue

One partner for products, equipment and the people to run them.

Most businesses end up juggling a cleaning company for staff, a supplier for chemicals, and another for equipment and mats. Because Safisha supplies all three, our services are built around the same products and machinery we already know inside out.

Contract & On-Site Cleaning

Regular, scheduled cleaning teams working at your premises — offices, clinics, retail floors, warehouses — so you're not managing cleaning staff yourself. Covers window cleaning, carpet cleaning, toilet deep cleaning and post-construction/builder's clean-up. Because we also supply the chemicals and equipment used on site, our teams work with products matched to your surfaces from day one.

Mat Rental & Laundering

Entrance and logo mats supplied on a rotation: we deliver clean mats on a set schedule, collect and launder the worn ones, and replace them — so your entrances stay presentable without you owning, storing or washing a single mat yourself.

Equipment Supply, Installation & Servicing

We don't just sell hand driers, dispensers, vacuum cleaners and mop systems — we install them and keep them running, so a faulty unit doesn't sit broken in a washroom for months waiting on a supplier who only does sales.

Product Consultation & Training

Because we supply the chemicals ourselves, we can advise on the right product and dilution rate for your surfaces, and train your own cleaning staff to use them safely and effectively — cutting waste and avoiding damage from the wrong chemical on the wrong surface.

Sanitary Waste Disposal

We already supply and service sanitary bins — this extends that into a scheduled collection service: used contents are collected, sterilised and disposed of correctly, so washroom hygiene doesn't stop at the bin itself.

Why one supplier

Most businesses split this across three vendors. We don't make you.

A cleaning company knows your site but not the chemicals. A products supplier knows the chemicals but not your site. Safisha is built to be both, so the people cleaning your premises and the products they're using come from the same place.

The usual way

A cleaning contractor for staff, a separate shop for chemicals, another supplier for mats and equipment — three invoices, three relationships to manage.

With Safisha

One supplier for the products, the matting, the equipment and the people who use them — consistent quality end to end.

What that means for you

Fewer vendors to coordinate, staff trained on the exact products you're using, and equipment that gets serviced rather than just sold.
